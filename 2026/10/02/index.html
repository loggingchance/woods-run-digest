Mills, Markets & Procurement

CANADA — BRITISH COLUMBIA / PULP CAPACITY

Howe Sound pulp mill is set to restart after B.C.-Domtar agreement

Domtar's Howe Sound pulp mill in Port Mellon is expected to return to regular operations in mid-October after an agreement with British Columbia changed the course of the August indefinite-idling plan. Unifor says the restart remains contingent on Local 1119 members ratifying a new collective agreement next week.

Domtar said the curtailment would remove about 380,000 metric tonnes of annual NBSK capacity and affect about 400 employees.

Watch: Union ratification is the remaining near-term gate.
