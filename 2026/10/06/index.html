NATIONAL — LOGGING SAFETY / NEW FIELD TOOL

Free Woods-Ready Safety Plan app puts job-site emergency information into one shareable plan

The Northeastern Loggers’ Association is offering the Woods-Ready Safety Plan, or WRSP, as a free browser-based tool to help logging crews assemble and share essential emergency information before work begins. Development was supported by a grant from the National Timber Harvesting and Transportation Safety Foundation, administered by the Forest Resources Association, and the app is available free regardless of location.

WRSP brings site coordinates, written responder directions, contact information, access restrictions, hazards and emergency-equipment details into a single plan. Users can mark the site location and enter directions that can be read directly to a 911 operator. The app runs in a web browser and can be saved to a phone’s home screen without an app-store download.

Once a plan is built, WRSP produces a readable one-page PDF and matching image for printing or sharing by email or messaging. Plans are stored locally on the device, and saved plans plus the built-in Quick Guide remain available offline after the app’s files have loaded; online maps and address lookups still require a connection. The app was created by Dr. Steven Bick, CF of Northeast Forests LLC.

Feedback is built into the tool through More > Send Feedback, and the project explicitly treats WRSP as a preparedness aid rather than a substitute for safety training, calling 911 or following local emergency procedures.

Two Pennsylvania Game Commission sales totaling 1,213.7 MBF open today

The Pennsylvania Game Commission lists two hardwood sales for bid opening today, October 6. State Game Lands 44 in Elk County offers 379.5 MBF plus 244 tons of mixed hardwoods, while State Game Lands 64 in Potter County offers 834.2 MBF plus 199 tons.

WEST — WYOMING / MILL CLOSURE

Bearlodge Forest Products says it will close October 31 after nearly 50 years

Bearlodge Forest Products in Hulett, Wyoming, says it will close its sawmill and pallet operations after October 31, ending a nearly five-decade run. The company cited high input costs, a limited labor pool, scarce housing and sustained downward pricing trends rather than a single cause.

The closure matters beyond the plant itself because the business occupies a small-diameter and pallet-market niche and supports equipment, parts, welding and other local spending. Wyoming officials also continue to point to timber-supply constraints and efforts to expand Good Neighbor Authority work as part of the broader operating environment for the region’s mills.

NATIONAL — FOREST CARBON / DATA TOOL

New FACT beta platform links forest inventory and harvested-wood-product carbon data

The U.S. Forest Service and U.S. Endowment for Forestry and Communities have launched a beta version of Forestry Analytics for Carbon Tracking, or FACT, a free platform intended to combine public and private U.S. forest and harvested-wood-product carbon data in one system.

Forest managers can use the platform to convert inventories into carbon stocks and estimate carbon for smaller areas, while manufacturers can use it to calculate and report carbon impacts from harvested wood products. The beta is open now, with Version 1 planned for summer 2027.
