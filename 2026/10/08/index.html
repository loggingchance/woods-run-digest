NORTH AMERICA — MASS TIMBER / COMPANY FINANCES

Mercer defers $25.8 million interest payment; mass-timber plants keep operating

Mercer International said in an October 2 filing that it had elected not to make the approximately $25.8 million interest payment due October 1 on its 2028 notes, using a contractual 30-day grace period. The company emphasized that this step is not itself an event of default. An independent board committee is negotiating a debt recapitalization and capital infusion with a group holding more than 75% of its outstanding 2028 and 2029 notes.

Mercer said its pulp, lumber and mass-timber operations continue in the ordinary course, with customers served and suppliers, contractors and employees paid. Negotiations remain unresolved, and the company said no agreement or timetable is assured.

Mercer’s three mass-timber operations in Washington, Arkansas and British Columbia account for an estimated 30% of North American cross-laminated timber capacity. For customers and suppliers, this is a material financial risk to monitor, not a reported plant shutdown.

NORTHEAST — MAINE / TIMBER BIDS

Army’s Aroostook County timber sale closes at 2 p.m. Eastern today

The Corps of Engineers is taking sealed bids for Butterfield Lake North standing timber at the Maine Army National Guard site in Caswell. Three harvest areas total about 268.6 acres. Listed volumes include 2,049 tons of balsam fir, 4,311 tons of aspen, 1,401 tons of mixed hardwood/firewood, 3,449 tons of mixed softwood pulp, and 1,607 tons of biomass.

Important bid logistics: the deadline is Thursday, October 8 at 2 p.m. Eastern, and sealed bids must reach the Corps real-estate office at 803 Front Street in Norfolk, Virginia—not the Maine harvest site. The invitation requires harvesting and hauling to finish by May 31, 2027 and specifies seasonal restrictions.

MIDWEST — INDIANA / TIMBER BIDS

Navy’s 281,190-board-foot Crane sale closes today

The Breakroom FY27 timber sale at Naval Weapons Station Crane covers two tracts totaling about 111 acres. The Navy lists approximately 877 trees with an estimated 281,190 board feet on the Doyle scale. Sealed bids are due Thursday, October 8 by 2 p.m. local time at NAVFAC Mid-Atlantic’s Building 2516 on the station.

The sale calls for a lump-sum or per-unit bid and gives the purchaser two years after award to complete the work. Review the IFB, maps and bid-security instructions; this is a sale of standing timber, not a contract for the Navy to pay for logging.

WEST — MONTANA / SALVAGE TIMBER BIDS

7,029 tons of wind-thrown Douglas-fir reach bid opening today

Montana DNRC lists the Luke Mountain 2 Salvage sale for October 8 bid opening in Missoula. The Anaconda Unit offering contains approximately 7,029 tons of wind-thrown Douglas-fir sawlogs, about 1,100 MBF Scribner net, across 482 acres. The prospectus specifies salvage treatment, deposits, harvesting methods and operating windows that will shape hauling costs.

DNRC also lists Case for Potomac for October 13 and additional Good Neighbor Authority sales later in the month. Check the current agency notices for amendments before bidding.

CANADA — ONTARIO / FUNDING

Forest-biomass funding application deadline: October 15

Ontario’s Forest Biomass Program accepts applications through October 15, 2026 at 11:59 p.m. Eastern. Streams cover biomass pathways and feasibility studies, innovative manufacturing, Indigenous partnerships and modernization of existing facilities. Eligible businesses should verify the matching stream and required supporting documentation.

The province encourages applicants to discuss projects before submission at forestbiomass@ontario.ca. A biomass outlet can change the economics of low-grade wood recovery and plant upgrades, but the grant is stream-specific rather than a general logging subsidy.

NATIONAL — HARDWOOD PLYWOOD / TRADE LAW

Two Chinese plywood exporters remain excluded from a 2018 antidumping order

An October 5 U.S. Court of International Trade order requires Commerce to keep Xuzhou Jiangyang Wood Industries and Zhejiang Dehua TB Import & Export outside the older hardwood-plywood antidumping order, with zero margins for those firms. The judge rejected Commerce’s attempt to reopen their individual examination after the appeals court affirmed their exclusion.

This is not a general elimination of the order: obligations for other firms can be very different. Buyers and domestic manufacturers should check the actual exporter and applicable case. A remand redetermination for the rest of the case is due October 26.

CANADA — MASS TIMBER / HOUSING MARKETS

Open building kit aims to make multiple mass-timber suppliers interchangeable

Perkins&Will and its partners have developed a non-proprietary system for six- to eight-storey timber apartment buildings. Their approach standardizes components to allow beams, columns and panels from different suppliers, reducing dependence on a single manufacturer. The research received a C$250,000 co-investment from Canadian digital-industry funding.

The open question is demand: mills will need repeat purchase orders before expanding production, while builders want dependable capacity before committing to wood. The proposal focuses on removing that coordination barrier rather than announcing a guaranteed new mill order.
