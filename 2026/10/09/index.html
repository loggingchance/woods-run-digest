Lead story

MONTANA — SPECIALTY SAWMILL / CLOSURE

Marks Lumber sets November 6 end to sawing as family business winds down

Marks Lumber in Clancy, Montana, says it will stop accepting special-cut orders on October 20 and cease sawing on November 6. The company plans to sell existing inventory through 2026 and 2027, with its final closing date dependent on remaining stock. The announcement marks a phased exit, not an immediate closure.

The specialty mill has supplied circle-sawn lumber, timbers, flooring and other wood products. For contractors, foresters, log suppliers and truckers, October 20 is the actionable custom-order deadline. The company's announcement emphasizes retirement and gratitude to workers and suppliers; it does not quantify lost production capacity.

TENNESSEE — FEDERAL TIMBER CONTRACT / DEADLINE

Arnold Air Force Base extends FY27 timber-harvest bids to October 16

The Air Force's Arnold AFB FY27 timber-harvest solicitation, notice FA910126QB076, has been amended. The current SAM.gov record puts submissions due at 1 p.m. Central on Friday, October 16, rather than the original October 9 date.

Amendment 0002 updates the evaluation factors to price only and provides questions and answers. The agency anticipates an October 23 award. Contractors should use the amended solicitation and its performance work statement rather than an earlier listing showing today's superseded deadline.

ONTARIO — FOREST BIOMASS / GRANT DEADLINE

Ontario forest-biomass applications close October 15

Ontario's Forest Biomass Program remains an approaching opportunity for companies developing low-grade fiber outlets, biomass pathways or facility improvements. The published application deadline is October 15, 2026, at 11:59 p.m. Eastern.

Applicants should confirm their stream and project eligibility directly with the province. The program is not a blanket subsidy for all timber harvesting, but qualifying utilization and innovation projects can improve markets for low-value material.

OREGON — STATE TIMBER / SALE CALENDAR

Oregon's next scheduled timber-sale bid week starts October 19

Oregon Department of Forestry lists October 19–23 as its next timber-sale bid-opening period, with sales conducted through district offices. Its current state-level listing shows no newly advertised district sales on the summary page.

The agency separately lists minimum-price offerings, including Cronin Too in the Astoria District and Miller Woods Thin in West Oregon. Interested buyers should inspect tract documents and district-specific notices before treating any listing as currently bid-ready.

Under the Radar

NATIONAL — FEDERAL TIMBER-SALE RULES

Forest Service clears obsolete purchaser-credit regulations

An October 7 final rule removes federal regulations for timber-sale purchaser credit under authority that expired in April 1999. The Forest Service describes the changes as technical and clarifying because no current contracts remain subject to the expired credit authority.

The amendments take effect November 5, 2026. Purchasers should not interpret this housekeeping rule as a new financing benefit or the elimination of an available current credit; its practical importance is in bringing the rulebook into line with longstanding law.

GREAT LAKES — FIBER DEMAND / INDUSTRY OUTLOOK

Industry meeting highlights Michigan paper investments and changing pulpwood markets

A Forest Resources Association summary published October 8 reports that Billerud leadership discussed investments at its Escanaba and Quinnesec mills and the potential for greater wood-fiber use. The discussion also highlighted fuel, equipment and labor pressures across the harvesting supply chain.

The meeting summary separately describes a market tension: timberland owners face weak pulpwood outlets even as certain paper and packaging assets seek new growth. These are management presentations and expectations, not newly announced purchase commitments to individual suppliers.
