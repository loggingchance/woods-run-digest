MIDWEST — SOUTHERN ILLINOIS / NOVEMBER 17 REPORT

Shawnee links timber output with forest improvement

Shawnee National Forest reports about 7.3 million board feet of timber generated during fiscal 2026, alongside stand improvement on more than 440 acres and invasive-plant treatment on more than 1,400 acres. Its November 17 account describes timber sales as one tool for improving habitat and forest regeneration.

The forest also reports prescribed burning on 13,297 acres. These are forest-wide accomplishments for the year ending September 30, not a new timber offering or a forecast of work available to bidders.

ROCKIES — MONTANA / APPLICATIONS DUE DECEMBER 4

Montana forestry grants reimburse community tree work

Montana’s Urban and Community Forestry Program Development grants accept applications through December 4. Requests range from US$5,000 to US$20,000, with a match requirement. Eligible applicants include local governments, community organizations, nonprofits, higher-education institutions and tribal governments.

The program supports canopy health, species diversity and landscape recovery; eligible costs can include contracted forestry, arboriculture and planning services. Funds reimburse documented expenses after recipients have paid them. Applicants should review the program’s match rules and consult their local state forester.

Under the Radar

NORTH AMERICA — TRUCK EQUIPMENT / LOW-SPEED POSITIONING

Volvo’s remote steering moves the driver outside the cab

Volvo’s VNX vocational truck can be equipped with Remote Steer, letting the driver position it with a handheld controller at up to 5 mph. Volvo included the feature in its October truck unveiling; November 17 reporting explains the dealer-enabled option and its equipment requirements.

The controller is paired to one truck’s identification number, works only within a limited distance, and has an emergency stop. This is driver-controlled positioning for tight jobsites, rather than autonomous hauling. Truck News lists the required steering, transmission, braking and body-builder systems.
