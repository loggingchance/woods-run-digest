CANADA — QUEBEC / SAWMILL OPERATIONS

Domtar to halt sawing at its Baie-Comeau mill November 20

Domtar told Quebec media that Scierie des Outardes will stop sawing on November 20 for an indefinite period. The company cited uncertainty over fibre supply and harvest planning, high fibre and freight costs, U.S. duties and weak lumber prices.

The pause reaches beyond lumber. The Journal de Québec reported that chips from the mill also go to Domtar’s Clermont pulp mill and Arbec’s Port-Cartier biofuel operation. The report did not identify replacement chip supplies or a restart date.

Trade Policy Watch

  • U.S. Commerce’s final antidumping and countervailing duty orders on hardwood and decorative plywood from China, Indonesia and Vietnam are scheduled for Federal Register publication November 17. The orders give importers producer- and exporter-specific cash deposit tables. They also distinguish covered panels from qualifying stamped structural softwood plywood and certain panels that failed structural certification, for which Commerce directs refunds of specified deposits. The notices explain which entry dates and scope rules apply. Antidumping order and deposit tables · Countervailing duty order and scope

U.S. WEST — NORTHERN IDAHO / FUELS WORK

Northern Idaho plans pile burns through December

The Bureau of Land Management lists prescribed pile burning near Pinehurst and across several sites in its Cottonwood field office area through December. At Happy Trails in Adams County, the plan covers roughly 257 acres of hand piles and 338 acres of machine piles. Burn timing depends on weather, staffing and field conditions.

U.S. — NATIONAL FORESTS / COMMENTS DUE DECEMBER 14

Forest Service seeks comments on targeted grazing permits

A November 12 Forest Service proposal would let the agency use temporary grazing or livestock permits for stewardship work, including targeted grazing. It also proposes a framework for changing grazing in response to drought or wildfire. Written comments are due December 14; the notice gives filing instructions under RIN 0596-AD75.
