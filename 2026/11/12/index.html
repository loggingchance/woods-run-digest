U.S. — WYOMING / NOVEMBER 6 REPORT

Bridger-Teton eyes a La Barge timber sale for summer 2027

The Bridger-Teton National Forest’s Kemmerer and Greys River districts are working with the Wyoming State Forestry Division on small timber projects under Good Neighbor Authority. In a November 6 SVI News interview, Kemmerer District Ranger Adam Calkins said he anticipates the first sale from the La Barge area in summer 2027.

Calkins said partners visited mills in Evanston and La Barge as well as potential work sites on the forest. Showing partners both ends of the supply chain helped bring some of them into the project. He also credited Lincoln County and Wyoming State Forestry for their cooperation.

Calkins described timber harvest as part of the districts’ effort to open dense stands and improve wildfire resilience. The summer 2027 date is the ranger’s planning estimate: the interview did not announce a bid opening, an award or a completed harvest.

U.S. — NORTH DAKOTA / FY2027 FUNDING CUTOFF NOVEMBER 13

North Dakota adds windbreak and tree planting options to pilot

Friday, November 13, is the cutoff for North Dakota producers seeking FY2027 funding under the USDA Natural Resources Conservation Service’s Regenerative Pilot Program. Applications are accepted year-round, but producers seeking funding in this fiscal year should apply by that date through their local NRCS Service Center.

NRCS’s October 27 announcement added Windbreak/Shelterbelt Establishment and Renovation (380) and Tree/Shrub Establishment (612) to the North Dakota options. The additions give landowners and conservation districts more practices to discuss when planning whole-farm projects. They can also inform project planning for nurseries and forestry service providers; NRCS determines eligibility and funding, and neither is automatic.

U.S. — OREGON / NOVEMBER 10 ANNOUNCEMENT

Oregon recognizes operators for protecting streams and stands

The Oregon Department of Forestry announced three regional Operators of the Year on November 10: Wood River Timber of Klamath Falls, Iron Horse Logging of Mapleton, and Lone Rock Logging of Roseburg. The eastern Oregon award cited Wood River’s work with small landowners to meet harvest objectives while reducing fire risk.

The department singled out Iron Horse for protecting buffers near a wetland and fish-bearing stream, and Lone Rock for planning cuts and changing cable corridors while protecting trees left in stream buffers. The awards recognize specific field work under Oregon’s forest practice rules.

Under the Radar

U.S. — SOUTH DAKOTA / STANDING RESOURCE

South Dakota lists cost-share for temporary forestry crossings

South Dakota’s Department of Agriculture and Natural Resources lists a standing Temporary Bridge Cost-Share Program for portable skidder bridges, bridge mats and other temporary crossing structures intended to limit streambed disturbance. Primary and secondary forest-products operators in South Dakota are eligible to apply.

The agency directs inquiries to its Natural Resources Planner in Rapid City. This is an existing program listing; the page does not give a current application deadline, available balance or cost-share rate.
