CANADA — NEW BRUNSWICK / NOVEMBER 20 DEVELOPMENT

Arbec talks open another route for Nackawic’s future

New Brunswick has entered a non-disclosure agreement with Arbec Forest Products to explore opportunities for the idled Nackawic mill, Unifor reported November 20. The union welcomed the discussions while emphasizing that workers’ livelihoods are not yet secure.

The talks are a new step beyond the province’s earlier timberland bid and examination of options for the facility. They do not establish a sale, a restart date or renewed wood purchasing. Unifor identifies contractors, forestry operations and local businesses among those affected by the mill’s future.

Forestry & Wood Products Profile

Angie and Frank Bonner built a business—and a place to learn

Frank Bonner got his start as a logging-business owner when Robert Bennett of Bennett Timber saw someone who could make the operation work. Angie handled bookkeeping, payroll and repair parts while holding another job, later joining the business full time. The couple, based in Evans, are Louisiana’s 2026 Outstanding Logger honorees. Jeff Zeringue’s November 3 Louisiana Forestry Association profile follows their work beyond the award. Angie learned to operate a skidder and helped organize regular Logging Council meetings so loggers could obtain continuing-education hours. Their daughter Jade learned equipment operation and now learns stand decisions from Frank: basal area, thinning rows and protection of streamside management zones. Frank hopes she will eventually take over the business. Their account connects succession with teaching the work in the woods, alongside the financial and organizational work that keeps an operation going.

U.S. — WOODWORKING EDUCATION / STANDING OPEN OPPORTUNITY

Fresh Wood welcomes student instruments and digital fabrication

The Association of Woodworking & Furnishings Suppliers is accepting free entries for its 2027 Fresh Wood student competition through April 15. The rules require eligible students to be at least 16 and younger than 35 by July 13, 2027, with instructor and school support.

Musical Instruments and Digital Fabrication are categories for the 2027 competition, held during the July 13–15 fair in Las Vegas. The October 15 call remains an open opportunity for eligible students and their instructors; it is not a newly launched grant or a general business competition. The organizer’s competition page provides the entry route and rules.

Under the Radar

U.S. — TREE PLANTING / NOVEMBER 18 SURVEY

Tree-planting interest runs ahead of confidence with a shovel

The Arbor Day Foundation’s 2026 Canopy Report finds that 58% of U.S. adults say they lack the knowledge to plant a tree alone. Eight in ten say they would plant one tomorrow if someone provided a tree and showed them how; four in five report not planting one in the past year.

Released November 18, the Harris Poll study represents more than 2,000 U.S. adults. It measures reported attitudes and behavior, rather than nursery orders, verified planting skills or promised participation. The findings identify a gap between interest in planting and confidence about carrying it out.
